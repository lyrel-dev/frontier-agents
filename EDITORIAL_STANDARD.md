# Editorial standard

## Explanatory sequence

Introduce the agent and its resource model before listing functions. Explain integration direction and authorization before describing data use. Explain how knowledge is produced, retained, selected, corrected, and removed before applying it to a use case.

## Prose and headings

Use descriptive sentence-case noun phrases, as in a technical book. Begin a section with the idea and its relationship to the preceding topic. Use procedures and tables where they answer configuration or comparison questions; do not substitute labeled fragments for an explanation. Catalog records are narrated situations, work, and interpretations with reference fields as a supplement.

## Outcomes and implementation relationships

Maintain each operational objective once. Every primary case has a responsible actor, completion criteria and connected narrative. Preserve separate cases when the result, decision or authorization boundary differs. Treat cadence, network route, identity and format as variants when the operational objective remains the same.

Workflow steps identify purpose, responsible actor, required/optional/conditional mechanism, its condition, concrete feature configuration, effect, acceptance evidence and failure handling. Human review is labeled as a team procedure rather than invented native behavior. A feature is a concrete service facility; its mechanism explains a documented process. The guide remains the detailed technical authority.

An implementation profile supplies reusable configuration choices and proposed acceptance exercises. It is not a deployable AWS schema or a claim that products share permissions, runtime or memory. Product-specific publication and mutation controls remain attached to the selected variant after consolidation.

Keep old identifiers and titles as aliases to the maintained successor. A split record offers all relevant successors rather than choosing silently. Alias data is navigation metadata, not an archived copy of superseded prose. Preserve searchable supporting references without counting them as primary outcomes.

Validate both directions: case to mechanism to feature, and feature to affected cases. Required dependencies may block the selected implementation; optional dependencies change its available context; conditional dependencies apply only to the declared profile. These modeled relationships do not constitute live account-readiness evidence.

## Diagrams

Provide named resources, directional relationships, a caption, accessible title and description, and linked source sections. Distinguish logical models from undisclosed implementation. Do not draw a shared runtime, memory store, or permission model without evidence.

## Product claims

Link material product behavior to official documentation. Separate native behavior, configured integrations, team-composed procedures, and synthetic evidence. Availability applies to the specific feature. Memory retrieval is not automatically model training; retention is not automatically backup and recovery.

## Presentations

Maintain HTML decks with visible chapter membership, substantive diagrams or comparisons, source-linked notes, and explicit exercise budgets. Choose content form according to the subject; no arbitrary required number of rows, cards, or procedure steps defines quality.

## Verification

Check source mapping and content consistency separately from HTML behavior. Check actual rendering, navigation, filters, figure accessibility, slide timing, and print restoration. Report what was tested and what was not. No live-service result may be inferred from a document build.
