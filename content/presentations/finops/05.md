1. Use the anomaly event or explicit question to establish the affected scope.
2. Inspect cost drivers and optional operational-change evidence through their actual source services.
3. Record reconciled contributions and remaining causal uncertainty before publication.

<!-- block: b-a28bfcddf73e5016808c -->
## Presenter notes

<!-- block: b-2d180b30c5ce51eaadb6 -->
An anomaly is a reason to investigate, not proof of cause. EventBridge matching, task creation, analytical completion and destination delivery are distinct inspection points. A nearby CloudTrail event is corroborating evidence only when its relation to the cost change is established.
