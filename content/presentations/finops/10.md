1. Retain the original recommendation source, scope, assumptions and estimated benefit.
2. The service owner reviews and implements an authorized change outside this analytical workflow.
3. Measure a comparable post-change period; observed savings are not the original estimate.

## Presenter notes

<!-- block: b-d6936295e99b5499a3e5 -->
Show the two separate objectives on the same path. First prioritize candidates; later measure the actual outcome of an implemented action. They use different evidence and are not one automatic optimization loop.
