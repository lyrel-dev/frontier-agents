1. An assessment produces findings associated with its inputs and run.
2. A repair proposal becomes a reviewed and deployed change through the application team.
3. Revalidation creates new evidence for selected findings; it is not broad new discovery.

<!-- block: b-f6f215b2a3ea554885d4 -->
## Presenter notes

<!-- block: b-19abbd134d3153429dac -->
These are linked work records, not assertions about an undocumented internal multi-agent graph. The team deployment step is external. The original finding remains distinct from the remediation pull request and subsequent revalidation job.
