Use cases/Planning and commitments

AWS FinOps Agent

Review commitment coverage

Assess coverage of eligible usage by existing commitments.

Responsible actor. Commitment planning owner

1. Purpose and scope

A commitment planning owner needs to understand how existing commitments cover eligible usage. Specify the commitment type, workload scope and observation period. Inspect coverage directly instead of inferring it from a spending forecast or total account cost.

Review the observation with service owners and distinguish a coverage gap from an instruction to buy. A purchasing decision has separate assumptions and authority. The case ends with a reviewed coverage assessment, not a transaction.

Inputs
Commitment type, observation period, eligible workload scope and supported coverage source.
Expected output
Reviewed commitment coverage observation.
Execution pattern
On demand
Delivery mode
Native function

2. Workflow

The workflow order, acceptance criteria and failure decisions are authored engineering guidance. Each mechanism links to documented service behavior and concrete configuration features.

  1. Read commitment evidence

    Inspect the supported commitment scope and period.

    Responsible actor. Service execution; accountable engineer reviews the result

    Dependency. Required. Applies to the selected implementation.

    Platform process. Source-backed cost analysis. Read the relevant upstream service and interpret its returned evidence.

    Configuration. Cost and operational source access

    Effect. Source read

    Verification. Reconcile a material result using the same dates, metric, scope and dimensions.

    Failure handling. Withhold unsupported numerical or causal conclusions and identify missing sources.

  2. Review coverage with service owners

    Consider workload constraints before a separate purchasing decision.

    Responsible actor. Responsible engineer or reviewer

    Dependency. Required. Applies to the selected implementation.

    Implementation. Engineering procedure; no native service operation is asserted for this step.

    Effect. Team decision; no implicit service action

    Verification. Record the decision with the source identifiers and evidence needed for the case completion criteria.

    Failure handling. Keep the result unresolved and assign the missing decision or evidence to an owner.

Setup procedure

  1. Verify the agent data role and relevant upstream service.
  2. State material query choices even when preferences are retained.
  3. Reconcile a material result independently and inspect task or artifact identity.

Proposed checks

  • Different filters invalidate a direct numerical comparison.
  • Forecasts and recommendation estimates are not added to observed costs.
  • Missing change evidence leaves causal attribution unresolved.

Stop behavior. Cancel tasks and manage artifacts separately. This recipe authorizes neither infrastructure changes nor commitment purchases.

Supporting procedures for this configuration

4. Verification and limits

Completion criteria

  • Commitment type, period and eligible scope are explicit.
  • Coverage observations are distinct from forecasts.
  • No purchasing authorization is implied.

Prerequisites and availability

Preview

Limitations

Coverage, utilization and forecasts answer different questions. Purchasing requires its own decision and authority.

Example request

Review existing commitment coverage for the selected usage and period without recommending or executing a purchase.

Verification procedures are proposed checks. This documentation does not report a live execution of the workflow.
