AWS FinOps Agent

Measure the outcome of an implemented optimization

Measure post-change cost and service behavior within defensible attribution limits.

Responsible actor. Change owner

1. Purpose and scope

An approved optimization has been implemented, and the owner wants to know what changed. Select comparable before-and-after periods and record traffic, pricing, allocation, and service-health differences that could affect the result. Reconcile the costs using the same metric and scope.

Report the observed change together with its attribution limits. A lower bill is not sufficient evidence that the optimization caused the decrease when demand also fell. This is a team measurement workflow using source-backed analysis, not a service guarantee of savings. Unexplained differences remain part of the outcome rather than being removed from the report.

Inputs
Implemented change record, comparable periods and service indicators.
Expected output
Measured outcome with attribution limits.
Execution pattern
Periodic after change
Delivery mode
Composed workflow

2. Workflow

The workflow order, acceptance criteria and failure decisions are authored engineering guidance. Each mechanism links to documented service behavior and concrete configuration features.

    Confirm deployment and select comparable observation periods.

    Responsible actor. Responsible engineer or reviewer

    Dependency. Required. Applies to the selected implementation.

    Implementation. Engineering procedure; no native service operation is asserted for this step.

    Effect. Team decision; no implicit service action

    Verification. Record the decision with the source identifiers and evidence needed for the case completion criteria.

    Failure handling. Keep the result unresolved and assign the missing decision or evidence to an owner.

  2. Read comparable cost evidence

    Reconcile values under the same analytical basis.

    Responsible actor. Service execution; accountable engineer reviews the result

    Dependency. Required. Applies to the selected implementation.

    Effect. Source read

    Verification. Reconcile a material result using the same dates, metric, scope and dimensions.

    Failure handling. Withhold unsupported numerical or causal conclusions and identify missing sources.

    Account for other changes before attributing the difference.

  1. Verify the agent data role and relevant upstream service.
  2. State material query choices even when preferences are retained.
  3. Reconcile a material result independently and inspect task or artifact identity.

Proposed checks

  • Different filters invalidate a direct numerical comparison.
  • Forecasts and recommendation estimates are not added to observed costs.
  • Missing change evidence leaves causal attribution unresolved.

Stop behavior. Cancel tasks and manage artifacts separately. This recipe authorizes neither infrastructure changes nor commitment purchases.

4. Verification and limits

Completion criteria

  • Before and after periods are comparable.
  • Demand, prices and allocation changes are disclosed.
  • Observed change is not replaced with a prior savings estimate.

Prerequisites and availability

Preview service; team process

Limitations

A lower bill does not prove the change caused the decrease; traffic, prices and allocation may differ.

Example request

Compare the post-change period with a matched baseline and state what cannot be attributed to the change.

Verification procedures are proposed checks. This documentation does not report a live execution of the workflow.
