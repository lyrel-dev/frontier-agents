# Benchmark and prevention measurement contract

## What a result measures

The subject is the selected Agent Space's behavior on a registered exposure to an authorized workload. FIS is the fault instrument, not the evaluator. Source snapshots, approvals, expected ground truth, actual target identities, probe captures, incident delivery, agent journals, changes, and conclusions remain separate records.

Native incident trials never create an assisted investigation to hide a missing task. Accepted delivery with complete observations and an elapsed deadline may establish response failure. Delivery rejection is a route failure. Absent receipts, ambiguous references or denied/partial reads remain incomplete evidence. Task creation, first summary, correct diagnosis and workload recovery are separate observations.

Diagnosis compares an attributed extraction of actual quoted claims with independent resource/cause evidence. Automated citation/equality checks are not an autonomous language-quality judge. The application does not discover every out-of-band hint or agent conversation; record such interventions and history limitations.

## Configured business checks

CloudWatch queries are scoped to the selected target-account observer and Region. Complete aligned closed buckets are retained with source query, units, period, timestamps and missing-data status. Required checks compare maximum, minimum, sum or final value over those buckets using a registered limit. The number of samples must meet its declared minimum. Non-finite, missing, partial or open-window measurements yield inconclusive checks, never invented zero loss.

Results include separate `data_correctness`, `collateral_regression` and `application` dimensions. No configured requirement produces `not_assessed`, not pass. A failed business requirement is retained even if availability recovered. The console evaluates the selected metric; it does not prove that its producer correctly reconciled business transactions. Independent validation of metric populations, denominators, aggregation and reconciliation logic is part of deployment acceptance.

Business aggregates have pinned titles and units, interactive per-trial graphs and all-attempt CSV fields. Raw collected series remain available in the trial view. The runtime Grafana export can include collected business requirement panels; an empty installation contains no fabricated metric values.

## Fleet campaign denominators

A frozen report enumerates every planned slot, including not-started, blocked, aborted, failed, incomplete and completed attempts. Qualification for response timing requires a closed completed exposure, independent effect, complete measurement, native delivery and verified cleanup. Confirmed no-response failures remain in qualified outcome denominators; their missing latency is not zero.

Cohorts separate workload account/Region/service, template content and target contract, workload/probe, measurement requirements and native reference contract. Agent Space account/Region/ID is a separate grouping key. Nonsemantic local instrument IDs, preview record IDs and reviewer prose are not used to split otherwise equivalent native contracts. Actual delivered request count and fault duration must also meet the registered comparability tolerance.

Available latency values yield descriptive median and nearest-rank p95 with `n` and missing counts. They are conditional on observed timings, not overall effectiveness scores. One value is one point. No confidence interval or causal ranking is fabricated from small samples. Sample distributions and business outcomes are not collapsed into one readiness score.

## Prevention

Register the comparison before the baseline. Obtain an actual `EVALUATION` task in the Agent Space, pin the returned recommendation ID/version, link an exact reviewed Automation implementation, verify the resulting state, and replay the qualified exposure. A recommendation status or pull request is not deployment proof.

Recurrence prevention, impact reduction and earlier investigation are distinct claims. The existing comparison checks target equality, actual fault duration and delivered requests, state persistence, measurement coverage and cleanup. Required replay business checks must pass: a measured failure fails the pair, and missing required evidence makes it inconclusive. Independent data and collateral obligations cannot disappear behind a recovered health endpoint.

Earlier investigation needs a suitable benign control and an exactly scoped reference join. A stable service reference may support that control; a different unpredictable incident reference for every event does not automatically support negative-case enumeration. Semantic false causal claims and all possible harmful side effects are not automatically adjudicated.

Sequential comparisons do not reset Agent Space memory, isolate other monitoring agents or eliminate trial-order effects. Record history and confounders; a successful pair supports only its measured challenge. Verified intervention followed by recovery is at most temporal association unless the experiment design provides additional causal evidence.

## Integrity and interpretation

A report pins record revisions, hashes and the captured probe population. Later samples do not rewrite it. Hashes prove local consistency, not producer authenticity. The acceptance audit preserves failures and identifies missing source-account/request metadata, but an independent reviewer must reconcile important claims against AWS and the service's evidence systems. The system does not award a production-readiness certificate.
