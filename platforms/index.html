How to read the collection

Capabilities identify resources and operations. Mechanisms explain triggers, process, state, configuration and controls. Verification exercises are proposed tests, not product functions or evidence of successful execution.

The reference decks are untimed. Each platform has an optional eight-minute prepared-result exercise, outside the existing scenario programme.

Official product documentation supports the product statements. Configuration steps are documented guidance; verification exercises are proposed and were not executed. Selected critical sources were rechecked on 21 October 2026; other links are retained from the preceding review. This is not an exhaustive service catalog, a fresh audit of every historical claim, or a live AWS test.
Version control: Git history. No manual archive copies or distribution packages are produced.
