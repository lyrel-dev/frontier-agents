## Build checks

The build checks local paths and anchors, unique HTML IDs, narrative completeness, all four presentation and demonstration budgets, presentation reference IDs, all 50 capability/pattern reference records, neutral wording in authored titles/takeaways, and PowerPoint existence. `qa/build-validation.json` records the structural results. Browser checks, when available, are recorded separately in `qa/browser-validation.json`; absence of that file does not imply they passed.

Demonstration budgets include prepared-result fallback paths and are not agent-runtime guarantees. The cost fixture is synthetic. No measured reliability, accuracy, security-detection, productivity or savings outcomes are claimed.
