## Consistency and lifecycle

This operation reads the local observation store. Its response can contain provider-like fields and application-owned metadata. Observation age, representation and provenance must remain visible to callers.

Pagination defaults to 20 results and permits at most 100. Continue with the returned opaque `nextToken` and unchanged filters. A local snapshot expires after 900 seconds; it is not a provider-wide atomic snapshot.
